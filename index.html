# Privacy Policy – FlashHunter **Last updated: September 2, 2026** ## 1. Introduction This Privacy Policy explains how FlashHunter handles information when you use the FlashHunter mobile application. FlashHunter is a lightning photography application developed by an independent developer. ## 2. Information handled by FlashHunter ### Camera FlashHunter requires access to your device's camera to allow you to photograph lightning strikes. Photos taken with FlashHunter are stored locally on your device. FlashHunter does not upload your photos to a server or store them in an online account. ### Photos and local storage FlashHunter may access or create photo files on your device in order to save and display photographs taken with the application. These photos remain on your device unless you choose to share, move, or delete them using features provided by your device or other applications. ### Personal information FlashHunter does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, or other information that you voluntarily provide to the application. FlashHunter does not operate its own server or backend for collecting user data. ## 3. Advertising FlashHunter displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about advertising interactions in accordance with Google's policies and the user's consent choices. FlashHunter uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent and advertising privacy choices where required. For more information about how Google handles data, please refer to Google's privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy ## 4. Data sharing FlashHunter does not sell your personal information. FlashHunter does not upload or share your photographs with third parties. Advertising-related information may be processed by Google AdMob as described above. ## 5. Data retention and deletion Photos created with FlashHunter are stored locally on your device. FlashHunter does not maintain an online database containing your photographs or personal information. You can delete photographs created by FlashHunter using the application's available deletion features or through the storage controls provided by your device. Advertising-related data may be retained by Google according to Google's applicable policies and retention practices. ## 6. Data security FlashHunter does not transmit your photographs to its own servers. Information handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob is subject to the security and privacy practices of those services. ## 7. Children's privacy FlashHunter is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children. ## 8. Changes to this Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to FlashHunter, its services, or applicable requirements. The latest version will always be available at this page. ## 9. Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy or FlashHunter's privacy practices, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the FlashHunter Google Play Store listing. **App: FlashHunter**
